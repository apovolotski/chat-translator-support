Privacy policy

Effective November 6, 2026 · Android package com.apovolotski.chattranslator

Messages, screenshots, and translation results are processed on your device. The app does not send that content to a translation server. Optional history is stored in private app storage, excluded from backup, and can be cleared here. History is off by default.

The translator keyboard processes only drafts you type in it. It does not read other apps’ messages and disables translation in password or private input fields. Images are accessed only when you select or share them.

Google ML Kit downloads language models and collects SDK diagnostics and usage information, including installation identifiers, device and app information, performance, errors, and configured languages. Google encrypts that telemetry in transit. No account, payment, advertising identifier, location, contacts, microphone, or accessibility permission is required.

Uninstalling removes app settings and local history. For privacy questions, contact apovolotski@gmail.com.

Data used by Google ML Kit

Google ML Kit processes message text and images on the device. The SDK sends diagnostics and usage telemetry to Google: per-installation identifiers, device and application information, performance and error information, feature usage, input/output size, and configured or identified languages. This telemetry supports analytics, diagnostics, SDK maintenance, and abuse prevention. It is encrypted in transit. The app does not sell your information, include advertising, or add third-party analytics SDKs. The developer does not receive the SDK telemetry or your message content.

Google determines retention and deletion of its SDK telemetry under its own privacy policy. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes the local settings and translation history. Contact us with privacy questions; for Google-held data, see Google’s privacy controls.

Your choices

Translation history is disabled by default. You may enable it, clear it, or turn it off to erase it. Downloaded language models may be deleted in the app. Enable the translator keyboard only if you want to use it; you can disable it in Android settings. No account is created. No server-side user profile or message history exists to delete.

Children and changes

This is a general-purpose utility intended for users aged 13 and above, not a service directed to children. We will update this page and its effective date if the app’s data practices change.

Support

Email apovolotski@gmail.com. Please include your Android version, app version, and a description of the problem. Avoid sending private conversations or sensitive screenshots.

To start, select your language and your conversation partner’s language, download the models over Wi-Fi, then write a reply or read an incoming message. Share selected text or a screenshot from another app to Chat Translator. Enable the optional keyboard in Android settings to translate your own drafts inside other apps.

Translation is powered by Google Translate using ML Kit on-device models: Google Translate technology. Machine translations may contain errors and should be reviewed for important conversations.

Independent utility. Not affiliated with WhatsApp or Meta. No translation subscription or API key is required.
